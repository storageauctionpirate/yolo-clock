About the numbers

Starting point: US Social Security period life table 2023 (2026 Trustees Report): remaining life expectancy for your age and sex. ssa.gov/oact/STATS/table4c6.html

Habit effects: verified rows from published studies: Doll 2004 & Jha 2013 (smoking, quitting), Zhuang 2026 (steps), Moore 2012 (exercise), Qian 2023 (sleep), Wood 2018 (alcohol), Veerman 2012 (TV), Prospective Studies Collaboration 2009 (BMI), Fadnes 2022 & Bellavia 2013 (diet). Capped by Nguyen (Million Veteran Program: 8 healthy habits ≈ +24 y men / +20.5 y women).

Where days go: BLS American Time Use Survey 2025; commute from Census ACS 2024 (27.2 min each way).

Limits: these are averages from observational studies; effects overlap and are shrunk for older ages. They describe groups of people, not you. Not medical advice.

The clock: your life from birth to your goal age is drawn as one day, midnight to midnight. The hour hand sits at your age, the gold marker where your current pace ends; the gray slice after it is time habits can win back.

Goal range: up to 122, the age reached by Jeanne Calment, the oldest verified human.
