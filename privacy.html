Privacy Policy

Covers Pirate Arcade (Storage Auction Scramble, Storage Stack, Storage Survival, Box Dig, and future mini games) and YOLO: The Life Clock (Life Clock, Life Jar, Look it up, Clean streak, Muscle fuel).
Published by Storage Auction Pirate LLC. Effective October 22, 2026.

The short version: No accounts. No sign-up. Your game progress and your clock data stay on your phone. We don't collect it, we can't see it, and we never sell it.

1. What the apps store, and where

Everything you enter or earn is saved in your device's local storage (the app's private storage on your phone):

  • Pirate Arcade: scores, best levels, unlocked units, in-game cash, campaign progress, and the player name you type for races.
  • YOLO Life Clock: the profile you enter (birth year or age, sex, height, weight, activity level), habits, routines, eating window, streak timers, food logs, and goals.

This data is never sent to us or to any server we run. We don't run any servers that receive your data.

2. No accounts, no tracking by us

There is no login, no email sign-up, and no profile in the cloud. We don't use analytics, crash trackers, or cookies to follow you. We don't sell, rent, or trade any data, and we don't share it with data brokers.

3. Health-style info in YOLO Life Clock

Numbers like weight, age, and habits stay on your device only. The life estimates are based on large population averages. They are for motivation, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before big changes to diet, fasting, or quitting a substance.

4. Online race mode (Storage Auction Scramble)

If you choose to join or host a 2-4 player race, your device connects directly to the other players' devices. To find each other, devices use a public connection broker that briefly sees your IP address and a temporary room code. Your race name and moves go only to the players in your room. Nothing is saved after the race. Solo play makes no network connection for this.

5. Purchases

If a paid upgrade (such as a Pirate Pass) is offered, payment is handled entirely by the app store you bought from. We never see or store your card or billing details. We only learn that a purchase was made so the app can unlock it.

6. Deleting your data

Use the reset option inside the app, clear the app's data in your phone settings, or uninstall the app. That permanently erases everything, because the only copy is on your device. We can't recover it for you.

7. Kids

These apps are rated for ages 13 and up and are not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Rewarded ads Applies once ads are added

This section takes effect only in a version of the app that shows ads. Current versions show no ads.

Some features may let you watch an optional ad for a reward (for example, an extra life or a bonus). Ads are served by a third-party ad provider. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, that provider may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (a resettable ID set by your phone's operating system),
  • IP address and approximate (city-level) location,
  • device type, operating system version, and app version,
  • ad interactions (whether an ad was shown, tapped, or completed).

That data goes to the ad provider under its own privacy policy, which we'll link here when ads launch. On iPhone you'll be asked for permission before any cross-app tracking; if you say no, you get non-personalized ads. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Buying a Pirate Pass removes ads (if offered). Your game progress and Life Clock data are never shared with the ad provider.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Big changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
